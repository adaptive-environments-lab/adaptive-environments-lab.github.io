Mission

A trans-disciplinary initiative designing places that promote human and ecological health amid accelerating environmental change.

Traditional design relies on prediction. Prediction fails when conditions change faster than infrastructure can adapt. The Lab takes a different path, with the designer as orchestrator rather than author.

The missing link is workflow. It connects persistent sensing and edge computing to small physical interventions, so a place can modify itself in response to what it observes.

Principles
01

Observational intelligence

Design decisions emerge from sustained attention to how environments perform. Sensing networks document conditions across scales and across time.

02

Micro-scale adaptation

Small adjustments informed by continuous observation aggregate into systemic change. The work moves with long-term trajectories, not against them.

03

Computational partnership

Simulation and machine learning augment human judgment, never replace it. Design stays accountable to the communities and ecologies it serves.
